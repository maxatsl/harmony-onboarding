Questions women ask us

Is Harmony medical advice?

No. Harmony does not prescribe, does not give dosing advice and does not replace your doctor. It plans your protein and training around whatever your doctor already prescribed, and gives you a report you can bring to your next appointment.

Do I need to be on a GLP‑1?

No. If you are not on a medication, your plan is built around your menopause stage. If you are, it also follows your injection week and, when you decide to stop, the weeks after your last dose.

What do I need for the strength test?

A sturdy chair, one flight of stairs and two shopping bags. It takes about five minutes at home. Skip any move that is not safe for you.

What is the Strength Score?

Harmony’s own score out of 100, built from three home tests: chair stands in 30 seconds, one flight of stairs, and carrying two bags. It is a way to track your progress, not a clinical assessment.

How do I cancel?

In one tap, from your account, at any time. We send a reminder before every renewal.
